How a report is placed

The cause comes from the report’s tenant, not from intakeTag (intake’s guess). It runs in #lib/kb/split, on top of causeOf in #lib/kb/causes:

  1. One id, one report: the export’s duplicate rows count once.
  2. parser: the tenant writes the day first (asked of Intl), and the shared parser reads every date month first. Day and month come out swapped.
  3. hint: the report came through the booking-v3 form, whose date field says MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first as told; the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not fix this.
  4. oneDayMove: the reports the desk listed in desk-notes.json. Their bodies show a date one day off, or the same meeting on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. That is a time-zone shift, not a swap, so it gets its own ticket. The desk still counts these reports under parser, where they are filed, and so does the table.
"Known. One issue, one fix queued." does not hold: the queued parser fix leaves the booking-v3 hint and the one-day move open.
